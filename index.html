About $ZECS & mint details

ZECS uses the ZB-20 protocol on Zcash. Each mint creates one signed event for 210 ZECS, anchored by a Zcash transaction. Only confirmed events count toward the 21,000,000 supply cap. No premine, no presale, and no team allocation.
