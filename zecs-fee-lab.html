Can we cut ZECS mint fees?

This test sends 1 zatoshi from your shielded balance back to your own Noir shielded address with a non-mint test memo. It does not register a ZECS mint and cannot create ZECS.

Current ZECS mint transactions typically use both Ironwood and Sapling and pay about 20,000 zatoshi. We are testing whether a same-pool shielded route can reduce that to about 10,000 zatoshi.
