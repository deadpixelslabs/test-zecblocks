How token deployment works

This transaction registers your ticker in the existing RH-20 contract. It creates no new smart contract and grants no owner mint, freeze, supply change or admin balance controls. New tokens start with zero minted supply and allow repeated public mints until sold out.

After confirmation, your token appears automatically in the directory. Its number follows the on-chain deployment order. Two competing registrations for the same ticker cannot both succeed. Minting and marketplace trading are separate features.
